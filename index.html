Fabrizio Cafolla

My name is Fabrizio Cafolla and I'm a DevOps & Platform Engineer. I design cloud architectures, automate CI/CD pipelines, and build the infrastructure that lets development teams ship reliably and move faster.

I work primarily with AWS and GCP, managing infrastructure as code through Terraform. My main languages arePython and Bash, with some TypeScript and Go. Over the years I've worked across very different contexts small and medium businesses, corporate banking, startup ecosystems which taught me that tools and processes need to adapt to the people and the context, not the other way around.

I believe open source is the real engine of technological progress, and I try to live that through projects and community work. I founded Italia Open-Sourceand its European spin-off, European OpenSource: a community-driven platform to map and connect open-source projects, developers, and organizations across Europe. I also serve as Community Manager of Latina in Tech, bringing quality tech events to my city and building connections between developers and companies.

Outside of work, I travel whenever I can often as a digital nomad. Many of these trips turn into guides and stories on my blog, where the technical side meets the personal one.

Talk

Platform Engineering and AI Agents: Spec-Driven development for IaC

In Platform Engineering, AI agents can speed up IaC management, but without clear boundaries, they can also amplify errors and operational impact. In this talk, we'll look at a spec-driven approach in which dedicated agents operate with specific identities, explicit guardrails, and a purpose-built toolchain (MCP, prompts, and focused skills). The goal is to use AI reliably to manage cloud-native infrastructure with greater control, traceability, and security.
